# Code of Conduct

Be respectful, constructive, and welcoming. Critique ideas and code, not people. Harassment, discrimination, threats, and sharing private information without consent are not acceptable.

This applies to project issues, pull requests, discussions, and other project spaces. Maintainers may remove harmful content or restrict participation when necessary.

Report concerns privately to [saul@saugar.dev](mailto:saul@saugar.dev). Reports will be handled with care and confidentiality where possible.
